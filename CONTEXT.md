# CONTEXT.md

The shared language of this project.

## Language

**Build**: The whole sequence in which the page assembles itself as the visitor scrolls.
_Avoid_: Animation, intro, demo

**Team**: Everyone shown assembling the page during the Build.
_Avoid_: Swarm, cast

**Teammate**: One member of the Team, shown as a labelled cursor. Named by job title, never as an invented person.
_Avoid_: User, collaborator

**Delegator**: The Teammate who represents Louis. Sets direction, writes briefs, and approves or rejects work, but makes no edits.
_Avoid_: Owner, director, lead

**Designer**: The Teammate who lays out type, colour and shapes, and acts on the Delegator's notes.

**Agent**: A Teammate that handles repetitive work, such as duplicating and filling project cards. One kind of Teammate, not a word for all of them.

**Backend**: The Teammate who replaces placeholders with real data.

**Brief**: A comment from the Delegator that asks a Teammate for work or changes.
_Avoid_: Prompt, task

**Spectator**: The visitor. Drives the Build by scrolling, and otherwise only watches or skips it.
_Avoid_: User, player
